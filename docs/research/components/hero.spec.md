# Hero.spec — `src/components/sections/Hero.tsx` (id="hero")

Data: `hero` (title, subtitle, exhibitsLabel, exhibits[{label, zh, href}], image, caption{name, role}).

Reference measurements (1440×900): section `relative overflow-hidden md:-mt-50 md:h-screen` (fills the first screen, header overlaps). Left half paper, **right half `bg-stone` from x=720 to the edge, full height**. Big title spans both halves at the top (y 65–210 on the reference, one line; ours is two lines because "EVERYDAY LUXURIES" is longer): `text-[13vw] laptop:text-hero font-medium leading-xs tracking-tightest uppercase`, positioned `absolute left-25 lg:left-30 top-65`, `data-animation="split" data-split="words, chars" data-ease="expo.out" data-duration="1.35"`. Subtitle (Chinese, `tc`) below the title: `text-2xl lg:text-3xl font-bold leading-tight` at `laptop:top-[18vw]` left column, `data-animation="split" data-split="lines" data-delay="0.2"`. Bottom of the left half: `hero.exhibitsLabel` as `Label` (`text-stone-deep`), then a list of the 4 exhibits — each row `flex items-center justify-between py-8 border-t border-ink/20` (last row also `border-b`), left text `text-base font-bold` + `tc` zh small, right a `dot`; rows are anchors (`href`) with `group` + `link-underline`; whole list `data-animation="moveUp" data-delay="0.4"`. Right half: the portrait image bottom-aligned, ~640px tall on desktop (`Picture` with `fill` inside a `relative h-[640px] w-[420px]` box at `absolute bottom-0 right-100`), wrapped in `<div data-animation="ambient-move" data-ambient-direction="x"><div data-ambient-box>…</div></div>` and the inner Picture wrapper `data-animation="parallax" data-scroll-speed="0.85"`. Caption `hero.caption.name` / `hero.caption.role` at `absolute left-30 bottom-30` of the right half, `text-xs font-bold leading-none`, two lines, `data-animation="moveUp" data-delay="0.15"`. A full-section overlay `<span class="pointer-events-none absolute inset-0 hidden laptop:block bg-ink" data-animation="fade" data-from="0" data-to="1" data-scrub data-end="bottom top" data-repeat>` sits above everything (z-10) so the hero darkens as it scrolls out. The section itself carries `data-animation="parallax" data-scroll-speed="5" data-start="top top" data-end="+=110vh" data-start-at="0"` (hero drifts down slower than the page).

Mobile (390×844): section min-height auto; title `text-hero-mobile` two lines in the paper area (`pt-30`), then a `bg-stone` block containing the subtitle (`text-2xl`, `tc`, `p-25`) and the portrait (`Picture` non-fill, full width), then the exhibits list on paper. No overlay/ambient needed (engine ignores ambient on touch).

Accessibility: h1 = title; the exhibits list is a `<nav aria-label="展區">` with `<ul>`.
