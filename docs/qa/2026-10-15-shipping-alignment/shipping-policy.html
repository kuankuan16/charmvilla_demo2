Shipping & Processing Times

In-stock orders ship within 1–2 business days. Delivery typically takes an additional 3–7 business days after dispatch. We do not dispatch orders on weekends or U.S. federal holidays.

Pre-order items ship on the schedule shown on their product pages, expected by the end of November 2026.

Christmas order deadline: for delivery before Christmas to the contiguous 48 states, please order by December 15, 2026.

Once your order ships, we will email your tracking information.

Pre-Order Payment & Cancellation

  • Pre-orders are charged in full when you place your order.
  • Pre-orders are expected to ship by the end of November 2026.
  • You may cancel a pre-order for a full refund before it ships. Please email us at us@charmvilla.com with your order number.
  • Once shipped, pre-orders follow our Teas & Consumable Items return policy.

Address Accuracy

Please ensure your shipping address is entered correctly at checkout. Charm Villa is not responsible for packages delivered to incorrect addresses provided by the customer, or for packages marked as "Delivered" by the carrier but not received.

US Shipping & Delivery Estimates

  • Complimentary Shipping: Complimentary shipping on orders of US$199 or more to the contiguous United States (48 states).
  • Standard Shipping: For orders under US$199, or orders not eligible for complimentary shipping, shipping rates are calculated and displayed at checkout based on your location and selected service.
  • We do not ship to Alaska or Hawaii.
  • APO/FPO/DPO addresses: At launch, we are not able to ship to military or diplomatic addresses.

Delivery Estimates & Carrier Delays

Please note that all delivery times are estimates provided by the carrier. While we always strive to pack and ship your items promptly from our California facility, unforeseen carrier delays, weather events, or high shipping volumes may occasionally occur. Once a package is handed over to the carrier, Charm Villa is not liable for transit delays.

Lost in Transit

In the unfortunate event that your package is lost in transit, please contact us at us@charmvilla.com. While we cannot take responsibility for carrier errors, we will work closely with the carrier on your behalf to locate your shipment or file a claim to provide a suitable resolution.
